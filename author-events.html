Author Event Guidelines & FAQ

Everything you need to know before visiting Aldergate Lane for an evening reading or signing session.

Yes! The vast majority of our author readings, signings, open mics, and story time sessions are completely free and open to the public. RSVPs are recommended so we can guarantee seating for all attendees.

To support visiting authors and independent bookselling, we request that at least one book signed during the evening be purchased at Aldergate Books. You are welcome to bring up to two backlist titles from home for signing alongside your new purchase.

VIP Salon passes ($75) include an intimate 3-course dinner with the visiting author in our mezzanine reading room before the public reading, front-row reserved velvet armchair seating, wine/cocktail service, and a signed numbered first-edition book.

Aldergate Books is fully step-free accessible with an elevator to the mezzanine level. Dedicated event parking is available at the Market Square Garage (2 minutes walk), with validation offered for VIP Salon patrons.

We welcome author suggestions and book club host requests! Contact our head bookseller via our Contact Page or speak to any bookseller in store.
