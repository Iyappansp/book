Our Story

Twenty-six years of putting the right book in the right hands.

Aldergate started as a single shelf in the back of a stationery shop. It's now a full room — but the instinct hasn't changed.

How We Started

A shelf that outgrew its corner

In 1999, our founder Eleanor Voss began stocking a single shelf of secondhand fiction beside the till of a stationery shop on Aldergate Lane. Regulars started asking for more than stationery — they wanted her opinion on what to read next.

Three years later, the stationery shop became a bookstore. The shelf became eleven sections. The opinion-giving never stopped — it's still the thing we're proudest of.

Mission & Vision

OUR MISSION

Connecting Readers with Books Endure

To foster a sanctuary for slow reading, deep curiosity, and genuine human connection. We curate every volume with intention, creating space for readers to discover stories that spark thought, solace, and imagination long after the final page.

  • Handpicked & thoughtfully curated shelves
  • Dedicated quiet reading nooks and armchairs
  • Accessible literary community for all readers
OUR VISION

A Thriving Haven for Independent Culture

To stand as a beacon of independent bookselling, expanding literary access while preserving the timeless magic of physical bookshops. We aim to nurture future generations of readers, authors, and thinkers through vibrant community events.

  • Championing independent & diverse authors
  • Sustainable & ethical bookselling practices
  • Preserving rare, out-of-print & collectible heritage

Our Timeline

Twenty-six years, briefly

1999 — The First Shelf

One shelf of secondhand fiction inside a stationery shop on Aldergate Lane.

2002 — Aldergate Books Opens

The stationery shop becomes a full bookstore with eleven sections.

2011 — The Reading Corner

We knock down a wall and add armchairs. Customers start staying for hours.

2018 — First Author Event

Our first reading and signing draws sixty people into a room built for thirty.

2025 — 480 Events and Counting

Monthly book clubs, poetry nights, and workshops fill the calendar year-round.
