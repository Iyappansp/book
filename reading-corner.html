The Reading Corner

Not a display, not decoration — an actual room for reading, with no purchase required and no clock on the wall.

Why We Built It

A room, not a photo opportunity

In 2011 we knocked down a wall and gave up prime retail space for six armchairs and a rug. Booksellers thought we were mad. Customers started staying for hours — and coming back the next day to finish.

The rule has stayed the same since day one: pull any book off any shelf and sit with it as long as you'd like. Buy it after, or don't. We'd rather you read it here than not at all.
